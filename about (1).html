About

Building a scientific platform in stages.

VOC Research World is being developed as a long-term platform for scientific education, research communication, laboratory knowledge, instrumentation and innovation.

Mission

Make scientific knowledge clearer, more useful and more connected to practical research and laboratory work.

Long-term direction

Grow from a digital knowledge platform into a research and innovation organization supported by collaborations, datasets, projects and eventually laboratory capacity.

Platform architecture

V5 is designed to evolve.

Now

Static GitHub Pages front end.

Next

API/CMS for authors, articles, media and projects.

Later

Authentication, editorial workflow, database, analytics and research infrastructure.
